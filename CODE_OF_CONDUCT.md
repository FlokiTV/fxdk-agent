# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation should remain technical, respectful, and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory behavior, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Collaboration

Disagreement about architecture or implementation is expected. Critique the work, provide evidence when possible, and keep discussion focused on the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers when possible. Do not use public issues to expose private or sensitive information.
